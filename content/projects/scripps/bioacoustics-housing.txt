## The challenge
Bioacoustics research at sea depends on hardware that holds up on a mooring. The Raspberry Pi module needed a housing that met rigid deployment criteria, and the new design had to be smaller than the models before it.

## What I did
- Developed the mount independently, to fit the criteria for mooring deployment.
- Produced technical drawings, models and integrated assemblies in SolidWorks.
- Selected the mechanical and electronic hardware and created the bill of materials (BOM).
- Assembled and soldered test circuitry, and built prototypes with 3D printing.
- Wrote a MATLAB script that turned timestamped acoustic data into visual waveforms, to help with bioacoustic identification and analysis.

## How it came together
I worked with a multidisciplinary team to iterate on and test configurations. Each round of prototypes informed the next, and the final prototype was fabricated at a quantifiably smaller size than the previous models.

I also took part in a rapid design and prototyping challenge, where I designed and fabricated an aquatic drogue.

## What I took away
Presenting the project outcomes to a technical audience pushed me to explain design choices clearly, not only to build them.

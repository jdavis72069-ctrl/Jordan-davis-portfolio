## The challenge
A compact module for the Raspberry Pi and its accompanying hardware was needed for deployment of a deep-sea, real-time whale tracking mooring. The fixture had to be sufficiently small enough to fit in the datalogger while firmly attached for pelagic deployment. 

## What I did
- Developed the mount to fit the criteria for mooring deployment.
- Collaborated with multidisciplinary team of biologists and engineers.
- Produced technical drawings, models and integrated assemblies in SolidWorks.
- Selected the mechanical and electronic hardware and created the bill of materials (BOM).
- Assembled and soldered test circuitry, and produced prototypes with 3D printing.
- Wrote a MATLAB script that turned timestamped acoustic data into visual waveforms, to help with bioacoustic identification and analysis.

## How it came together
Produced a final prototype with quantifiable size reduction compared to previous Pi modules. Presented prototype to technical audience.

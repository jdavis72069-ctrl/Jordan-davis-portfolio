# BELOW THE BLANK LINE: the description. A blank line starts a new paragraph. A line starting with "- " is a bullet.
Jordan designed a housing for a Raspberry Pi hardware module used in deep-sea bioacoustics research. She independently developed a mount that meets the rigid criteria of mooring deployment, then iterated on configurations with a multidisciplinary team.

The final prototype was fabricated with a quantitative size reduction compared to previous models, and she presented the project outcomes to a technical audience.

- Produced technical drawings, models, and integrated assemblies in SolidWorks.
- Selected mechanical and electronic hardware and created the bill of materials (BOM).
- Assembled and soldered test circuitry, and built prototypes with 3D printing.
- Took part in a rapid design and prototyping challenge to design and fabricate an aquatic drogue.
- Wrote a MATLAB script that converted timestamped acoustic data into visual waveforms, to aid bioacoustic identification and data analysis.

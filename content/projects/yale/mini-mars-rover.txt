## The challenge
The class project asked our team to design a miniature rover that could obtain and retain soil moisture data. The rover also needed a protective outer shell.

## What I did
- Created the exterior CAD model and its drawings.
- Designed and tested the protective outer shell.
- Helped teammates with soldering and assembly.

## The result
Our rover tied for second place in the class competition. The judges were NASA-affiliated.

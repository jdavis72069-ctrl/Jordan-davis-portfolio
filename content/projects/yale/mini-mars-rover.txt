# BELOW THE BLANK LINE: the description. A blank line starts a new paragraph. A line starting with "- " is a bullet.
Jordan worked with a team of three to design a miniature rover that can obtain and retain soil moisture data. The team tied for 2nd place in the class competition, judged by NASA-affiliated judges.

Jordan created the exterior CAD model and drawings, designed and tested the protective outer shell, and helped teammates with soldering and assembly.

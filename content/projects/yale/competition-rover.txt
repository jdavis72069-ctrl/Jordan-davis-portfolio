## The challenge
The Yale Rover team builds a rover for the University Rover Challenge. The drives sub-team is responsible for the parts that move the rover, so the chassis, the body and the suspension all had to fit together.

## What I did
- Designed the electronics, battery, and fan mounts to optimize chassis space and balance airflow and hardware protection. 
- Helped assemble the chassis.
- Developed the motor mounts in Onshape.
- Machined aluminum components.
- Performed structural calculations to determine suspension fastener selection.
- Researched rocker-bogie suspension systems to choose the best mechanism.

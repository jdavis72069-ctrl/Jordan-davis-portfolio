## The challenge
A wheelchair scale only helps if a wheelchair can get onto it. Our project focused on ramps that make the scale more accessible, and we checked their strength through analysis and testing.

## What I did
- Designed and produced the 3D-printable ramps in Onshape.
- Performed mechanical and structural analyses through hand calculations and FEA simulations.
- Mentored team members through CAD and static analysis workshops.
- Aided in the fabrication and testing of prototypes, and recorded project documentation.

## What I learned
Co-leading meant teaching as well as designing. Running the workshops made me explain my analysis clearly enough for others to follow and repeat it, which sharpened how I explain my own work.

## The challenge
The device had to aid the client in eating and drinking while balancing functionality and comfort.

## What I did
- Communicated with the client and organized in-person testing.
- Improved the initial prototype to reduce its bulk.
- Iterated on the connective strap to make it more comfortable for the client.
- Collaborated with team members on assembly, and documented our progress.

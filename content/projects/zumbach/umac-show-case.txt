## The challenge
The UMAC CI needed to be shown at trade shows. That meant it had to travel in a case and still be ready to use as soon as it was unpacked.

## What I did
- Designed the carrying case configuration for the UMAC CI ultrasonic precision measuring device, so it could be presented at shows.
- Designed a monitor mount for the display.
- Designed custom electronics padding to hold the equipment in place during transport.
- Worked in SolidWorks on the layout and drafting, and revised the design as the parts came together.

## Why the layout matters
An exhibition piece has to be easy to pack, carry and set up, often by someone who did not build it. I designed the case around the monitor and the padding so the setup stays consistent wherever it travels.

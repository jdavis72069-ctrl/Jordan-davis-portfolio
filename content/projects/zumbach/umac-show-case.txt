## The challenge
A show case configuration had to be compact, sufficiently durable for travel, and functional within temperature constraints. 

## What I did
- Designed a precision-fit air TPU configuration and custom egg crate cushion for the UMAC CI and accompanying hardware 
- Invented a custom monitor mount
- Created SolidWorks models and integrated assemblies
- Researched parts and materials to account for vibration, jostling, and overheating
- Produced BOM 

## Why the layout matters
The exhibition module had to fit within the Pelican 1660 travel case while balancing heating and vibration concerns. The module was designed to be accessible and usable by sales representatives for demos.

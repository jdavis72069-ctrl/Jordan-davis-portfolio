## The challenge
An integrated UMAC CI case had to be aesthetically appealing, simple to assemble, and sustainable in a factory environment. 


## What I did
- Designed the fixture as a commercial product for the UMAC CI and its accompanying display and sensors.
- Built engineering models in SolidWorks and turned them into design-for-manufacturing (DFM) drawings.
- Wrote thorough documentation and bills of materials (BOMs).
- Created several prototypes.

## Keeping the team in the loop
Progress was presented at weekly engineering meetings with senior engineers.

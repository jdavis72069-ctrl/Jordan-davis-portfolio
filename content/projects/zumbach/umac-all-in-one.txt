## The challenge
The UMAC CI is an ultrasonic measuring system, and its display and sensors needed a dedicated fixture. Because the fixture was a product people could buy, it had to be easy to set up and make sense on its own.

## What I did
- Designed the fixture as a for-purchase product for the UMAC CI and its accompanying display and sensors.
- Built engineering models in SolidWorks and turned them into design-for-manufacturing (DFM) drawings.
- Wrote thorough documentation and bills of materials (BOMs), so the design could be built and ordered.
- Created several prototypes to check how the parts fit together.

## Keeping the team in the loop
I presented my progress at the weekly engineering meetings. Those meetings kept the design moving and gave everyone a clear view of where it stood.

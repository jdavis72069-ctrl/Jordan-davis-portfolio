## The challenge
The project was a device that harvests wind energy. Efficiency was the measure that mattered, and the design had to turn wind into power as effectively as possible.

## What I did
- Managed the project as project manager.
- Created the pinwheel-style design.
- Produced CAD models of the design.
- Fabricated prototypes from wood.

## What I learned
Moving from CAD models to wooden prototypes showed me how a drawing and a physical build can differ. I now check my models against the real parts as early as I can.

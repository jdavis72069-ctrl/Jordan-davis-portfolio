## The challenge
The project was a claw arm powered by hydraulics, and I was responsible for taking it from an idea to a working prototype.

## What I did
- Invented the gear and grabbing mechanism.
- Designed the gearbox in Fusion360.
- Fabricated the prototypes and refined them after each round.

## What I learned
As the project manager, I had to keep the whole build in view while working on one part at a time. The gears, the grabbing mechanism and the hydraulics all had to work together.

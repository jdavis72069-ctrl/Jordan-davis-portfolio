## The challenge
The device senses soil moisture to decide when plants need water. Beyond how it works, I wanted its look to feel natural and calm.

## What I did
- Led the project as project manager.
- Designed a wave-inspired, natural look for the device.
- Modeled the design in Fusion360 and produced its drawings.

## How I approached the design
I wanted the device to look calm and natural, so I drew its shape from waves. Turning that idea into a model in Fusion360 meant balancing how the device looks with how it has to be built.

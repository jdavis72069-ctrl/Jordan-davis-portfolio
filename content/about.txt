I'm a senior at Yale studying Mechanical Engineering. I'm most drawn to fluid analysis and product optimization, especially where they meet aerospace and ocean systems.

I also make art, and it shapes how I design. I look for the point where a part works well, looks clean, and stays simple to build. Over seven years of project management, CAD modeling, fabrication and iterative design have given me a steady habit of building, testing and revising.

My hands-on work runs from CAD modeling and documentation to CFD and FEA simulation, data acquisition and sensor integration, and building hardware with machining tools and electronics. I expect to finish my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (ABET) at Yale in May 2027.

Jordan Davis is a senior at Yale studying Mechanical Engineering. She has been captivated by the final frontiers of outer space and the deep ocean since a young age. She has a strong passion for fluid analysis and product design with aerospace and oceanographic applications. 

Her perspective as a visual artist equips her with a distinctive design approach that balances aestheticism and functionality through a creative problem-solving lens. 

She brings over seven years of CAD modeling experience and a strong fabrication background in 3D printing, machining, prototyping, and circuit assembly. She expects to finish her B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (ABET) at Yale in May 2027.

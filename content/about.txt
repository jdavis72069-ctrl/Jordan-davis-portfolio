Jordan Davis is a senior at Yale studying Mechanical Engineering, with a passion for fluid analysis and product optimization in aerospace and oceanographic applications.

As a visual artist, she brings a unique design perspective that balances functionality, aesthetic considerations, and simplicity. She has over seven years of experience in project management, CAD modeling, fabrication, and iterative design.

Her experience spans CAD modeling and documentation, CFD and FEA simulation, data acquisition and sensor integration, and hands-on hardware development with machining tools and electronics. She expects to earn her B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (ABET) from Yale in May 2027.
